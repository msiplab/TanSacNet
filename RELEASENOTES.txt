* Release notes on Tangent Space Adaptive Control Networks (TanSacNet) Package

- Unreleased (after v0.2.0)
-- (Major) Changed the coefficient order in each group of the 3-D block DCT/IDCT to that of getMatrixE0_ in the test cases (depth fastest, vertical slowest)
   This affects strides with two or more coefficients along multiple directions in a group, e.g., [4 4 2], [2 4 4] and [4 4 4]. Outputs for [1 1 1], [2 2 2] and [1 2 4] are unchanged.
--- tansacnet.lsun.lsunBlockDct3dLayer
--- tansacnet.lsun.lsunBlockIdct3dLayer
-- (Major) Revised the PyTorch block DCT/IDCT modules to split images into 2-D blocks and to order coefficients as in tansacnet.lsun
   The previous modules formed blocks from consecutive pixels in raster order and ordered coefficients row-major in each group. The PyTorch modules and analysis/synthesis networks now give the same outputs as the MATLAB layers for the same input. permuteDctCoefs and permuteIdctCoefs were replaced with dctmtx and block_dct_matrix_2d in lsunUtility.
